Pricing questions

How much do home services cost with IServe?

Each service page and the pricing page list indicative ranges in PKR. They are planning estimates, so the final charge depends on the job, inspection, materials and requirements, and you confirm the quotation before work begins.

Are parts and materials included in the price?

Not unless your quotation says so. Items marked labor only exclude parts or materials, and for other items you should ask for materials to be listed in the quotation.

Why are prices shown as ranges?

Jobs differ in size, access and condition. A range gives a realistic idea before inspection, and the confirmed quotation replaces it.

How do I get a quotation?

Send your request through the booking form, WhatsApp or phone. The team contacts you to confirm the details, and you confirm the quotation before work begins.
