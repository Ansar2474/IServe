Questions about AC repair and service

How often should an AC be serviced?

Many manufacturers advise servicing before each cooling season, and more often in dusty homes. Your unit’s manual is the best guide.

Is a gas refill always needed when an AC stops cooling?

No. Poor cooling can come from dirty filters, a failing fan or a leak. An inspection finds the cause before any gas is added.

Why is my AC not cooling properly?

Common causes include dirty filters, blocked airflow, a failing fan or capacitor, or a refrigerant leak. Book an inspection so the cause is identified before any repair or gas refill.

What is included in AC servicing?

Servicing normally covers cleaning the filters and coils and checking cooling performance. The exact scope is confirmed in your quotation.

Why is water dripping from my indoor AC unit?

A blocked drain line or dirty filters are common reasons. An inspection will show whether it needs cleaning or a repair.
