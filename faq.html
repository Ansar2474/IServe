AC repair and service

How often should an AC be serviced?

Many manufacturers advise servicing before each cooling season, and more often in dusty homes. Your unit’s manual is the best guide.

Is a gas refill always needed when an AC stops cooling?

No. Poor cooling can come from dirty filters, a failing fan or a leak. An inspection finds the cause before any gas is added.

Why is my AC not cooling properly?

Common causes include dirty filters, blocked airflow, a failing fan or capacitor, or a refrigerant leak. Book an inspection so the cause is identified before any repair or gas refill.

What is included in AC servicing?

Servicing normally covers cleaning the filters and coils and checking cooling performance. The exact scope is confirmed in your quotation.

Why is water dripping from my indoor AC unit?

A blocked drain line or dirty filters are common reasons. An inspection will show whether it needs cleaning or a repair.

Plumbing

Can I book just an inspection?

Yes. An inspection identifies the problem, after which you can confirm the quotation for the repair.

Does the price include replacement parts?

Not unless the quotation says so. Ask for parts to be listed when you confirm the quotation.

What should I do about a leaking pipe before the plumber arrives?

If it is safe, close the nearest isolating valve or the main water supply valve and move belongings away from the water. Then book an inspection.

What does drain cleaning involve?

It aims to clear blockages in kitchen or bathroom drains. How severe the blockage is and how easy the drain is to reach affect the price, and the scope is confirmed in your quotation.

Why does my toilet keep running after flushing?

A worn flush valve or a misadjusted float is a frequent cause. A short inspection can confirm which part needs repair.

Electrical services

Should I keep using a socket that sparks?

Stop using it and switch off the circuit if you can do so safely, then book an inspection.

Do you install fans I have already bought?

Fan installation is a listed job. Mention that you already have the fan when you book.

How do I know a socket or switch needs replacing?

Heat, discoloration, a burning smell, buzzing or a loose fit are warning signs. Stop using it, switch off the circuit if you can do so safely, and book an inspection.

Can I replace a switch or socket myself?

Electrical work carries shock and fire risks. If you are not trained, switch off at the breaker and book an electrician.

Why does my circuit breaker keep tripping?

An overloaded circuit, a faulty appliance or damaged wiring can all trip a breaker. Unplug recent devices and book an inspection if it continues.

Home cleaning

What is the difference between home cleaning and deep cleaning?

General cleaning is for upkeep. Deep cleaning is a longer, more thorough job for homes that need a reset or have not been cleaned in a long time.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

What does kitchen cleaning cover?

It focuses on the kitchen surfaces and fittings that collect grease and grime. The exact scope is confirmed when you book.

How should I prepare my home before a cleaning visit?

Put away valuables and fragile items and tell the team about delicate surfaces or areas that need extra attention.
